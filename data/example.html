Banyans Surf Guide

Banyans is one of the most popular breaks in the Kailua-Kona area, for both its central location and the fact that it is a multi-personality A-frame that breaks year-round. The usual summertime S to SW angled swells march straight in with nothing in the way, while the steeper S to especially the more rare SSE-SE swells will have a harder time bending in.
